Ship like you mean it.

Your personal productivity dashboard on top of GitHub: the PRs you opened and merged, how many were approved on the first review, how long they waited, and your assigned issues on a board you can drag to update.

Read access to Metadata, Pull requests, Issues and Projects is enough. Add Projects write to move issues on the board.

  • We don't collect any data. There is no account, no tracking and no analytics.
  • There is no database and no server. Your browser or phone talks to GitHub directly.
  • Nothing is processed on our side. Your token and stats stay on your device, and you can replace or remove them at any time.
